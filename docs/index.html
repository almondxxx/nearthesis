Does NEAR Intents volume lead NEAR network usage better than price does?

Hypothesis under test: cross-chain volume through NEAR Intents is a better leading indicator of real network usage than the NEAR token price. This page tests that claim. It reports what the data says, including anything that cuts against it.

Lead-lag: does this week's change predict usage k weeks later?

The y-axis is the correlation between the predictor's weekly growth and usage growth k weeks later. Filled markers mean p < 0.05. A leading indicator needs significant bars at k ≥ 1, not just at k = 0.

Predictive test with BTC as the market control

"p" tests whether two weekly lags of the predictor add information beyond usage's own history and BTC. "Out-of-sample" is the forecast error for the next week with the predictor, divided by the error without it, using only data before each forecast. Below 1 means the predictor helps.

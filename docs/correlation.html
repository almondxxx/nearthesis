Which leads NEAR adoption: Intents volume or NEAR price?

Hypothesis: cross-chain volume through NEAR Intents is a better leading indicator of real network usage than the NEAR token price. This page puts both predictors against the same adoption metrics and asks two things: does each one move together with adoption in the same week, and does it move first? Every statistic uses week-on-week growth. Two series that merely trend over the same period would otherwise look related even if they aren't.
